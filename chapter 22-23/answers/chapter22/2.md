# Chapter 22, Challenge 2: Self-Referential Initializers

The local form `var a = a;` is a compile-time error in this interpreter. The
compiler sees the local declaration before compiling its initializer and
rejects a read of that local before initialization. This is safer than
allowing an indeterminate value. Globals retain their normal runtime lookup
behavior because the REPL may define a global on a later line.
