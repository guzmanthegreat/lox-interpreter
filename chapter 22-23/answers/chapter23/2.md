# Chapter 23, Challenge 2: `continue`

`continue;` is compiled as a backward jump to the nearest enclosing loop. The
compiler records the loop's continue target and lexical scope depth. Before
jumping, it emits the required `OP_POP` or `OP_CLOSE_UPVALUE` instructions for
locals declared inside the loop body.

For a `for` loop with an increment clause, the target is the increment code;
otherwise it is the loop condition. A continue outside a loop is a compile
error.
